# Outdoor Activities Images

This folder contains images for your outdoor activities page.

## Photo Gallery Images

Add 6 photos showcasing your outdoor adventures:

- **photo1.jpg** - Running/marathon photo
- **photo2.jpg** - Cycling photo
- **photo3.jpg** - Camping photo
- **photo4.jpg** - Trail hiking photo
- **photo5.jpg** - Mountain/summit view
- **photo6.jpg** - General outdoor exploration

**Recommended size**: 800x600px (4:3 aspect ratio)
**Format**: JPG or PNG
**File size**: Keep under 300KB each for fast loading

## Tips

- Choose photos that tell a story
- Mix action shots with scenic views
- Ensure good lighting and composition
- Consider adding captions in the HTML to provide context
- Compress images using tools like TinyPNG before uploading
