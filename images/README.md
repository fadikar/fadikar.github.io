# Image Guidelines

## Profile Photo (`headshot.jpg`)
- **Recommended size**: 400x400px (square)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **File size**: Keep under 200KB for fast loading
- **Style**: Professional headshot with neutral background

## Paper Images (`papers/`)
- **Recommended size**: 800x400px (2:1 aspect ratio)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Naming**: `paper1.jpg`, `paper2.jpg`, etc.
- **Content**: Representative figures, graphs, or visualizations from your papers

## Research Project Images (`research/`)
- **Recommended size**: 800x500px
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Naming**: `project1.jpg`, `project2.jpg`, etc.
- **Content**: Key visualizations or diagrams from your research

## Optimization Tips
- Use online tools like TinyPNG or ImageOptim to compress images
- Convert to WebP format for even better performance (requires updating HTML)
- Ensure images are properly credited if they're not your own work
