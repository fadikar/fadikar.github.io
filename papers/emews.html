Distributed Model Exploration with EMEWS

Nicholson Collier, Justin M. Wozniak, Arindam Fadikar, Abby Stevens, and Jonathan Ozik

2024 Winter Simulation Conference (WSC), pp. 72-86, 2024

IEEE Xplore

Abstract

This paper presents distributed model exploration with EMEWS, a workflow framework for large-scale, adaptive simulation studies.

EMEWS integrates workflow orchestration with high-performance and heterogeneous computing resources to support parallel optimization, calibration, and uncertainty-aware model analysis.

The framework enables scalable experimentation for complex scientific models, including epidemiological workflows that require iterative and computationally expensive search procedures.
